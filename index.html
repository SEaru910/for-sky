FOR SKY • ALWAYS & UNCONDITIONALLY

For you, entirely.

hi hello sky,
i hope life learns to be a little gentler with you, ang dami mo nang kinailangang intindihin, ang dami mo nang pinagdaanan na hindi naman dapat ikaw lang ang nagdadala. and i wish you could see how proud i am of you, not only for the days when you’re doing well, but especially for the days when everything feels a little too much and you still find a way to carry on. i hope you never measure your worth by how well you manage to hold everything together. you are just as deserving of love when you’re tired, when you’re drained, when you’re annoyed, when you make mistakes, even when you don’t feel pretty or when you have nothing left to give. hindi nababawasan yung halaga mo sa mga araw na hindi mo kayang ibigay yung best version ng sarili mo.
i appreciate your presence in ways ordinary words could never quite explain. kahit simpleng kwento mo, random thoughts, little updates, or just having you around, they all mean more to me than you know. i don’t need you to be anything other than yourself to be someone i cherish. and as for you, i’ll do everything i can to love you sincerely, without making you question where you stand with me. i’ll give you the kind of love that doesn’t have to be begged for, the kind that shows up even in the little things, and the kind that makes you feel just as cherished on your hardest days as you do on your happiest ones. you shouldn’t have to ask twice to be understood, appreciated, or reminded that you’re loved
if there are days when you forget how much you’re worth, let me remind you in all the little ways i can. i’ll love you in the moments you feel most lovable, and just as much in the moments you don’t. i’m so, so proud of you, sky. mahal na mahal kita, even in all the versions of yourself you’re still learning to understand.
with all my care, patience, and love, Forever here for you
